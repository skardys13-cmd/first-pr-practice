# VC / PE / M&A Glossary

Course terms only. `EXTERNAL CONTEXT` marks anything added from outside.

**Accredited investor** — Required for private-market investing because the SEC
cannot regulate every private entity, "so it requires accredited investors…
because they should know better than to get taken." $250K+ salary or $1M+ net
worth. *`SRC-P-001`, `SRC-P-014`*

**Accretive / Dilutive** — A deal is accretive when combined EPS exceeds the
acquirer's standalone EPS. The most common first test in M&A. *`SRC-P-013`*

**Add-on** — A complementary acquisition onto a platform company; typically
retains its identity. Bolt-on and tuck-in are synonyms. *`SRC-P-011`*

**Adjusted EBITDA** — EBITDA with one-time costs, management fees and
non-recurring items added back. Negotiated and heavily contested; it sets the
purchase price. *`SRC-P-008`*

**Alpha** — Return above what beta predicts. Positive = manager skill; zero = no
better than an index fund; negative = destroys value. *`SRC-P-013`*

**Anti-dilution** — Protects investors in a down round. Full ratchet (most
stringent) vs weighted average (most common). *`SRC-P-005`*

**Beta** — Volatility relative to a benchmark. β>1 more volatile, β<1 more
defensive. *`SRC-P-013`*

**Bootstrapping** — Funding from personal savings, sweat equity and early
revenue. Maximum control retained; forces lean operations. *`SRC-P-002`*

**Breakup fee** — Paid when a party terminates a deal. Qualcomm paid NXP $2B;
AT&T paid T-Mobile ~$4B. *`SRC-P-012`, `SRC-P-014`*

**Carried interest** — The GP's 20% of profits above return of capital (VC) or a
hurdle rate (PE). Taxed at 15–20% capital gains rates. *`SRC-P-001`, `SRC-P-011`*

**Convert point** — Where preferred is better off converting to common. The right
is in the term sheet. *`SRC-P-005`*

**Convertible note** — Unpriced debt convertible to preferred at the next round's
price. Senior to preferred. Dividends accrue but are typically unpaid.
*`SRC-P-005`, `SRC-P-003`*

**Corporate venture capital (CVC)** — Corporate investing arms. Over half of all
VC. Less valuation sensitive because they buy strategic value. *`SRC-P-006`*

**Dis-synergies** — Integration costs, customer defection, attrition. "Often
underestimated in models." *`SRC-P-012`*

**Divestiture** — Selling assets or divisions to focus, reduce debt, satisfy
regulators, or shed underperformers. *`SRC-P-011`*

**Dry powder** — Uncommitted capital. **$3.7T globally in early 2026**, over 40%
of it "aged." *`SRC-P-012`*

**Earnout** — Price contingent on future performance. Bridges valuation gaps;
30–40% are litigated ("sue-outs"). *`SRC-P-013`*

**EBITDA** — Net income plus interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. "The
most commonly used private equity measure." *`SRC-P-008`*

**Enterprise value** — Market cap + total debt − cash. The true cost to own the
whole business, capital-structure neutral. *`SRC-P-008`*

**Financial engineering** — Returns driven by leverage rather than operating
improvement. *`SRC-P-001`*

**General Partner (GP)** — The fund manager. Takes 2% fees and 20% carry; commits
1% of the fund. *`SRC-P-002`*

**Goodwill** — Purchase price minus fair value of net identifiable assets. Not
amortized; hits earnings only when impaired. *`SRC-P-013`*

**Greenshoe** — Underwriters' right to sell up to 15% more shares. *`SRC-P-007`*

**Hurdle rate** — Minimum return before carry is paid. *`SRC-P-013`*

**IRR** — Annualized compound return. "Basically the % return to the investor."
*`SRC-P-001`*

**Junk bond** — Rated BB or below. Yields ~5% above quality bonds; ~2% above what
an equivalent borrower would pay. *`SRC-P-008`, `SRC-P-010`*

**LBO** — Acquisition financed largely with debt that the acquired company
repays. Typically 1/3 equity, 2/3 debt. *`SRC-P-008`*

**Limited Partner (LP)** — Pension funds, endowments, insurers, family offices,
sovereign wealth, corporate venture arms. Provides the capital. *`SRC-P-002`*

**Liquidation preference** — Preferred's right to be paid back first. 1x
non-participating is standard. Preferences cumulate by round; forfeited on IPO
conversion. *`SRC-P-005`*

**MOIC** — Exit proceeds ÷ capital invested. "How many times did the money grow."
*`SRC-P-001`*

**Platform** — The first acquisition onto which add-ons are built. ~30% of PE
deals. *`SRC-P-011`*

**Post-money / Pre-money** — Value after / before the investment.
`PRE + MONEY = POST`. *`SRC-P-007`*

**Private credit** — Non-bank direct lending. $2T market funding 77% of LBO debt.
Bilateral, illiquid, floating rate, covenanted — *not* junk bonds. *`SRC-P-010`*

**Pro rata rights** — Right to maintain ownership in future rounds. "You can't
kick them out of the syndicate." *`SRC-P-005`*

**Protective provisions** — Preferred rights above common, including approval
requirements. *`SRC-P-005`*

**Quality of Earnings (QoE)** — Diligence analysis separating recurring revenue
from one-time or seasonal sources. *`SRC-P-012`*

**REIT** — Must distribute 90% of taxable income and hold 75%+ in real estate;
avoids double taxation. *`SRC-P-014`*

**Roll-up** — An add-on strategy replicating a cookie-cutter model for rapid
scale. *`SRC-P-011`*

**Rule of 40** — Revenue growth% + margin% ≥ 40%, using FCF or EBITDA.
*`SRC-P-007`*

**SAFE** — Simple Agreement For Equity. A warrant, not debt; buys preferred at
the next round's price at ~15% discount; indefinite term. *`SRC-P-005`*

**Secondary market** — Trading existing fund commitments or direct shares; a
liquidity "release valve" before exit. *`SRC-P-012`*

**Shelving / Withdrawing** — Putting an IPO on indefinite hold vs formally
terminating it. *`SRC-P-007`*

**S-1** — The IPO registration statement/prospectus. Discloses business,
financials, **cap table**, option pool, risks, use of proceeds. *`SRC-P-007`*

**Spin-off** — A division becomes independent; existing shareholders receive
shares; no cash changes hands. PayPal from eBay. *`SRC-P-013`*

**Teaser** — The succinct first document bankers use to shop a deal.
*`SRC-P-011`*

**Term sheet** — Where price and preferred terms are negotiated. "Not
understanding the term sheet is the number one source of later issues."
*`SRC-P-002`*

**Valley of death** — The unprofitable period VC finances a company through; PE
does not. *`SRC-P-001`*

**WACC** — Blended cost of equity and debt; the hurdle rate and DCF discount
rate. *`SRC-P-009`*
