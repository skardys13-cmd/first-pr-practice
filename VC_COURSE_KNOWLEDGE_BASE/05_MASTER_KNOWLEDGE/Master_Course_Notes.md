## 2. THE TWO COURSES

| Code | Course | Decks here |
|---|---|---|
| **FIN/ENTRP 4350** | Private Markets: Venture Capital, Private Equity and Mergers & Acquisitions | 13 of 14 |
| **FIN/ENTRP 4310** | New Venture Finance | **1** (`SRC-P-006`) |

`SOURCE FACT` (`SRC-P-001`) — 4350 course summary: *"This course delves into the
dynamic world of private market investing, exploring the critical roles in
driving innovation and economic growth…or not….."*

The trailing "…or not….." is preserved deliberately. The course takes a
consistently skeptical posture toward private-market self-justification, which
recurs in the PE material (`SRC-P-011`).

### 4350 course objectives — `SOURCE FACT` (`SRC-P-001`)

- Fundamentals of private market investing — private equity, venture capital,
  hedge funds, REITs, infrastructure, and **use of leverage**
- The similarities and differences — *"similar but mostly different"*
- The process and the ideal targets
- How investment opportunities are **perceived**
- How investment opportunities are **valued**
- The merger, acquisition and divestiture process:
  - How M&A fits into the private market
  - The M&A process — deal sourcing, valuation, negotiation, and integration

### Course arc — `CLAUDE INFERENCE` from class plans in `SRC-P-005/008/009/010`

The 4350 sequence runs: **VC → IPO/exit → Private Equity (growth) → LBO /
leveraged deals / private credit → M&A (incl. accretion/dilution) → the rest of
alternatives (hedge funds, REITs, crypto, commodities)**. Assessment is by
quizzes, "Ripped from the Headlines" article write-ups, and film-based cases.

**The single 4310 deck is not a course.** New Venture Finance is represented
here only by its Corporate VC lecture. That course's material is substantially
**missing** — the largest known gap in this project.
