## 3. THE THREE STRUCTURAL INSIGHTS

These are worth more than any individual fact, and none is stated in one place —
each emerged from reading across decks.

### A. The fund clock explains nearly all VC behavior
```
Fund life:                10 years        (SRC-P-002)
Time to IPO:              ~8 years        (SRC-P-002)
Capital to get there:     $75M+, 3+ rounds
Deal find → close:        0.5 year        (SRC-P-007)
```
**There is almost no slack.** Everything else falls out of it: the exit pressure
founders report (`SRC-P-007`), why VCs leave boards at exit, why ~90% of
companies are acquired rather than taken public, and why the VC Method assumes a
4-year hold.

### B. The instrument trade has exactly two axes
Priced preferred, convertible note and SAFE differ on: **must you price the
company today**, and **where do you sit in the payout stack**. Every other detail
— the discount, the seniority, the indefinite term — follows from those two.

### C. Leverage vs operations is the PE story
**RJR Nabisco** (95% debt, ~10–11x entry, ~14% IRR) against **Vista/Marketo**
(22% debt, operational fix, 3.1x in 25 months). Same asset class, opposite
method. And **"12 is the new 5"** (`SRC-P-012`) explains why the second has
displaced the first: without cheap debt, PE needs 12% EBITDA growth where 5% once
sufficed.
