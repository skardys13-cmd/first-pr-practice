# [COMPANY] — Investment Memo
**Prepared by:** · **Date:** · **Round:** · **Recommendation:** INVEST / PASS / TRACK

## 1. Summary & Recommendation
The ask, the price, the ownership, the expected return, and the decision — in
under half a page.

## 2. The Problem *(required)*
**"NAIL THIS CHART!"** What problem, and why does solving it create a
**high-growth** opportunity?

## 3. The Solution & Product *(required)*
Including key differentiators.

## 4. Market *(required)*
Size · growth rate — **must far exceed ~2% US GDP** · validation evidence ·
**Why Now?** (*"easy to see if something is right; very hard to time your
investment right"*)

## 5. Competitive Advantage *(required)*
And **Underlying Magic / IP** *(required)* — patents applied for, what is
genuinely unique.

## 6. Business Model *(required)*
How money is made per unit or per subscriber, then taken to scale.
**"A Business Model is not a Financial Plan…but it feeds it."**

## 7. Traction & Milestones *(required)*

## 8. Team *(required)*
Note: assume the founder may not be CEO at exit — **only 41% are.**

## 9. Go-to-Market *(required)*
**Name the partners.** They are both the distribution channel and **the most
likely acquirers.**

## 10. Financials *(required — two exhibits)*
- **Financials 1** — the full spreadsheet
- **Financials 2** — **revenue vs net income, showing the valley of death**
- Gross margin and its structural drivers
- **Cash plan covering all three needs: operating losses + working capital + capex**

Rounds to date, instruments used, current ownership, option pool.

## 13. Valuation
**Primary — the Venture Capital Method:**
```
Exit revenue estimate  ×  sector exit multiple  =  exit value
Exit value  ÷  required MOIC  =  maximum post-money supportable
```
**Cross-check:** market averages for this round and sector.
**Note:** DCF is not used at this stage — there is often no cash flow.

## 14. Dilution & Waterfall
Ownership at exit under this round plus expected future rounds. **Model a
mediocre exit**: does the preference stack leave common with anything?

## 16. Exit Analysis
Named potential acquirers (start with GTM partners) · IPO plausibility against
the **$75M+ / 3+ rounds / ~8 years** benchmark · **~90% are acquired** · does the
timeline fit the fund's remaining life?

## 17. Diligence Findings
Per `Due_Diligence_Framework.md`. Explicitly address: customer concentration,
quality of earnings, covenants, forecast credibility, IP ownership, key-person
risk.

## 18. Risks
And what would have to be true for each to become fatal.

What you would need answered before committing.
