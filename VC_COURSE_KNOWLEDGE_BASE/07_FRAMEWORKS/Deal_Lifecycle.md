## VENTURE DEAL LIFECYCLE

```
SOURCING → SCREENING → TERM SHEET → DILIGENCE → CLOSE
   → BOARD/PORTFOLIO INVOLVEMENT → FOLLOW-ON → EXIT
```

**Deal find, diligence and close: ~0.5 year** (`SRC-P-007`).

### The pitch (`SRC-P-006`) — 15 of 22 sections are mandatory
Required: Problem · Solution · Product · Business Model · Market Validation ·
Traction/Milestones · Competitive Advantage · Market Size · Underlying Magic/IP ·
Leadership Team · Go-to-Market · **Financials 1 (full spreadsheet)** ·
**Financials 2 (revenue vs net income — "shows your Valley of Death")** ·
The Ask · Thank You/Contact.
Optional: Value Proposition · Press/Testimonials · Competition Research · Board
of Advisors · Use of Funds · Summary · Appendix.

> **"NAIL THIS CHART!"** — on the Problem slide.
> A venture market must grow far faster than **~2% US GDP**.

### The term sheet — what gets negotiated (`SRC-P-005`)
**Economics:** price per share (set by pre/post money) · liquidation preference
(1x non-participating is standard; participating, 1x with a 2x cap, and 3x
non-participating all exist) · anti-dilution (**weighted average** common, **full
ratchet** punitive) · vesting and acceleration.
**Control:** protective provisions · board/director approval rights ·
registration rights (**"they can vote to make the company register for an IPO"**)
· pro rata (**"you can't kick them out of the syndicate"**) · information rights
· right of first refusal · co-sale/tag-along.

> **"Not understanding the Term Sheet is the NUMBER ONE source of later issues
> with founders and investors being misaligned, especially on EXITS."**

### Exit (`SRC-P-001`)
**"THERE IS NO PUBLIC MARKET and PRICING for PRIVATE COMPANIES. So you need AN
EXIT to get your investment and return back."**
IPO · Merger · Acquisition · Sale of the Company · Sale of the Assets · Sale of IP.

**At IPO, preferred automatically converts to common and liquidation preferences
are forfeited** (`SRC-P-005`) — the protection vanishes in the best outcome.

## PE DEAL LIFECYCLE

```
PICK A MARKET → FIND THE REFERENCE MULTIPLE → SCREEN THE BOTTOM
   → SOURCE (proprietary > banker) → LOI → DILIGENCE → FINANCE → CLOSE
   → CHANGE IT / LEVER IT → ADD-ONS → EXIT
```

### Selection (`SRC-P-011`)
Pick a space with multiple players and a highly-valued incumbent — *"THAT WILL BE
YOUR REFERENCE for 'GOOD'"* → note what companies trade for (**Market Cap/EBITDA
or EV/EBITDA**) → screen the bottom of the list → **"IS THERE EBITDA? ANYTHING
STAND OUT AS BROKEN OR NOT SCALING? FIX EM. Or ROLL THEM UP."**

### Sourcing (`SRC-P-011`)
**Proprietary beats banker-sourced**, because bankers run an auction that bids
the price up. Bankers are the *second* most common source and need **~$1M in
fees** to run a process. A **teaser** is the first document.

**Deal size segments:** Large Cap $1B+ · Upper Middle $500M–$1B · Middle
$100M–$500M · Lower Middle $50M–$100M · Small Cap <$50M.

### Value creation — the three levers, quantified (`SRC-P-010`, Vista/Marketo)
| Lever | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth / EBITDA expansion | **~45%** |
| Multiple expansion (strategic premium) | **~35%** |
| Margin improvement | **~20%** |

Plus **debt paydown**, which alone returned 3.75x on a zero-growth LBO
(`SRC-P-008`).

### The rollup (`SRC-P-011`)
Platform deals ~30% of PE transactions; **over 60% including add-ons**; **>70% by
dollar value in 2023**. Add-ons are cheaper, lower risk, attract **less LP
scrutiny**, and can close in as little as 2 months. Returns **~20% IRR vs ~15%**.
> **"'Flip' the neighborhood ….not just the House"**

## M&A DEAL LIFECYCLE (`SRC-P-012`)

```
STRATEGY (build / buy / borrow) → TARGET → LOI (exclusive)
   → DILIGENCE (virtual data room) → TERM SHEET (non-binding except exclusivity)
   → DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT (binding, must be publicly disclosed)
   → REGULATORY (HSR, 30-day wait, possible Second Request)
   → CLOSE → INTEGRATION
```

> **"A publicly known plan, that may or may not happen. CAN AFFECT THE BUSINESS!"**

**M&A term sheet:** purchase price (+ working capital peg) *same as VC* ·
**employee matters** *new* · **holdback/escrow, 8–15% for 1–2 years** *new* ·
exclusivity (**the only binding part**) · confidentiality.

**Antitrust:** HSR threshold **$126.4M** · 30-day initial waiting period ·
**2–5% of deals need remedies** · breakup fees (**Qualcomm paid NXP $2B**;
**AT&T paid T-Mobile ~$4B** — $3B cash + $1B spectrum).

**The base rate:** **47% of deals destroy value; ~73% fail to beat the S&P;
acquirers lose 4–7% over three years while targets gain 20–25%.**

## IPO LIFECYCLE (`SRC-P-007`)

```
Decision → select underwriters → S-1 filing & SEC review → roadshow (~2 weeks)
   → book building → pricing (24–48h after roadshow) → trading next morning
   → lock-up (90–180 days) → expiration
```
**Total: 18–24 months.** Spread **5–7%**; greenshoe **15%**.
**Oversubscription:** <1x red flag · 3–5x healthy · 5x+ prices 10–20% above range
· banks want **5–10x**. **The pop is engineered** — deliberate 10–25%
underpricing; 50%+ means money was left on the table.
**Readiness screen: Rule of 40.**
