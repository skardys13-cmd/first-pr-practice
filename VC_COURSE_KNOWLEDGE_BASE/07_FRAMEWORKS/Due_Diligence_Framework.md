## When it happens (`SRC-P-012`)
> *"Typically, Due Diligence follows the signing of an **exclusive Letter of
> Intent**. Similar to a VC Term Sheet….but the **LOI for M&A is typically more
> in depth** — adding terms for personnel issues/retention, compensation.
> **A Virtual Data Room is the tool used for this stage.**"*

> **"The most critical phase — uncovers risks that change price or kill deals."**

## The three core workstreams

| **Financial DD** | **Legal DD** | **Operational DD** |
|---|---|---|
| Audit of financials **3–5 years** | Contracts & commitments | Operational efficiency metrics |
| **Quality of Earnings (QoE)** | Pending/threatened litigation | **Customer concentration risk** |
| Working capital normalization | IP ownership & patents | Supplier dependencies |
| **Off-balance-sheet liabilities** | Regulatory compliance history | IT systems & cybersecurity |
| Revenue recognition policies | **Change-of-control provisions** | **Key employee retention risk** |

Third parties are *"often hired"* for legal and operational, and *"sometimes for
Quality of Earnings."*

## ⭐ The four financial DD items — each with a real failure attached

1. **Quality of Earnings** — *"distinguish recurring revenue from one-time or
   seasonal sources."* Verify margin sustainability.
2. **Working Capital** — *"excessive reliance on short-term debt can escalate
   financial risk."* → **`EV + WC = TOTAL NEEDED TO BUY AND RUN`**
3. **Debt Structure** — interest rates, covenants, maturities.
   → **`COVENANTS YANK LOANS IF CASH TOO LOW/SLOW…DANGEROUS…`** *(Picolight)*
4. **Forecast Accuracy** — *"overly optimistic forecasts can inflate
   valuations."* *(Occam)*

## The data room checklist (`SRC-P-012`)
**Corporate/Legal** — incorporation docs, bylaws, org charts, shareholder
agreements, litigation
**Financial** — 3 years audited, AR/AP/inventory, **CapEx history and forecasts**
**Market/Commercial** — market research, customer contracts, **churn rates**,
competitive analysis
**Operational** — workflows, supply chain, QC certifications, inventory turnover
**Technology/IP** — architecture diagrams, security policies, patents/trademarks/
licenses, roadmap
**HR** — management bios, comp and benefits, **turnover rates**, non-competes
**Risk** — insurance incl. cybersecurity, business continuity, **ESG**
**Growth** — product launches, **M&A targets under consideration**, cost programs

## Commercial DD
Market sizing and growth · competitive landscape (**"independent interviews with
customers"**) · **customer mix** (*"heavy reliance on a handful of customers can
create vulnerability if a large account leaves"*) · pricing vs value delivered

## IT / Technology DD
Infrastructure and security · scalability under projected growth · **technical
debt / field liabilities** · IT governance, data protection, disaster recovery

## Management DD
Track record and industry expertise · operational improvement opportunities ·
SEC/regulatory compliance · **cultural fit** (*"culture clashes often lead to high
employee turnover and lost productivity"*)

## Synergy testing (`SRC-P-012`)
> **"M&A is only rational if 1 + 1 > 2"**

| Type | Realization |
|---|---|
| **Cost synergies** | **12–24 months. "More predictable"** |
| **Revenue synergies** | **3–5 years. "Harder to achieve"** |
| Financial synergies | Lower cost of capital, NOLs, debt capacity |
| **Dis-synergies** | Integration cost, customer defection, attrition — **"often underestimated"** |

**Test it:** use the break-even synergy table (`Formula_Library.md`). At a 20%
premium you need **$38M** of synergies just to be EPS-neutral; at 50%, **$207M**.
*"Ask: is this synergy target achievable and credible? If no — the deal may be
value-destructive."*

|---|---|

*"Restricted/No Due Diligence"*, *"Excitement over the deal led to

## Red flags, consolidated
- Revenue concentrated in a few customers
- Recurring revenue overstated in QoE
- Working capital scaling faster than sales — **Guitar Center: inventory grew
  123% CAGR vs 15.8% sales CAGR** (`SRC-P-009`)
- Covenants that trip on a cash dip
- Forecasts requiring heroic assumptions
- Goodwill from prior acquisitions already impaired
- Synergies needed to justify the price that are *revenue* synergies
- Cultural mismatch, and no plan for day-one systems integration
