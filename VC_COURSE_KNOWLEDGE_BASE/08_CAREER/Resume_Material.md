> convertible notes and SAFEs; modeled multi-round dilution scenarios

## Phrasing rules
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Analyzed, evaluated, modeled, assessed, built, authored | Invested, deployed, sourced deals, led, executed |
| "in coursework" / "independently" | Vague phrasing implying professional context |
| Specific numbers from real exercises | Invented metrics |

**Never imply the RIS role involves private markets.** It is client-facing
retirement income work — valuable, and honestly described.
