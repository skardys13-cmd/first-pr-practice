# Portfolio Project Plan

**Version:** v1.0 — 2026-08-12. Ordered by **return on effort**.

> These exist to close the gaps in `Career_Gap_Analysis.md` — above all
> **modeling**, which is the single biggest concrete gap, and **written work
> product**, the accepted substitute for deal experience.

---

## Project 1 — Cap Table & Dilution Model ⭐ *do this first*
**Closes:** modeling gap · proves the strongest coursework claim
**Effort:** a weekend

Rebuild `SRC-P-005`'s exercise in Excel from scratch: founders at $0.01 par →
ESOP → Series A at $12M pre raising $18M → Series B at $100M pre raising $22M →
IPO floating 18.1%. Then build the one-round alternative and reproduce the
**$16.9M vs $12.4M per founder** result.

Then extend beyond the course: add a **liquidation preference waterfall** with
1x non-participating, participating, and capped participating toggles, and a
**down-round anti-dilution** switch for weighted average vs full ratchet.

**Why first:** it converts a coursework claim into a demonstrable artifact, and
it's the model most directly relevant to a VC analyst role.

## Project 2 — Investment Memos (4–6 companies)
**Closes:** no deal experience · no written work product
**Effort:** ~1 per month

Use `07_FRAMEWORKS/Investment_Memo_Template.md`. Real companies, ideally
Iowa/Midwest. Apply the **VC Method** to derive a supportable post-money and take
a clear position — including passes. A well-reasoned pass is as impressive as a
yes.

**Write these fresh.** (Seth has opted not to reuse the coursework memos.)
Start with companies you already have a view on — Iowa/Midwest, fintech,
insurance-adjacent. **One good memo beats five rushed ones.**

## Project 3 — Iowa Private Capital Thesis ⭐ *the differentiator*
**Closes:** no sector depth · no differentiated view
**Effort:** 2–3 weeks

Write 3–5 pages on **private credit's role in Iowa retirement and insurance
capital.** Anchor on the Global Atlantic/KKR example, the concentration of Iowa
IPOs in insurance and financial services, and what you see client-side at RIS.

**No other candidate has this.** It is local, current, and personally grounded.
Publishing it (LinkedIn, or a short Substack) makes it findable.

## Project 4 — LBO Model
**Closes:** modeling · PE/corp dev credibility
**Effort:** a weekend

Rebuild the zero-growth LBO: $100M entry, $80M debt, $15M sponsor, $5M
management, exit at $100M in year 7 → **3.75x / 4x**. Add a debt schedule,
leverage and coverage covenants, and sensitivities on entry multiple, leverage,
and exit multiple.

## Project 5 — Deal Log
**Closes:** sourcing gap · network
**Effort:** 30 minutes weekly, ongoing

Track Iowa/Midwest startups: company, sector, stage, round, investors, thesis,
your view. After six months this is both evidence of sourcing habit and a real
conversation opener with any regional investor.

## Project 6 — Accretion/Dilution Model
**Closes:** corp dev readiness *(the highest-reachability role)*
**Effort:** a day

Rebuild `SRC-P-013`'s three-step model with the break-even synergy table as a
live output.
