## The differentiated angle — Iowa private capital

This is the part no generic candidate can copy.

**Three things connect:**
1. **`SRC-P-010`** — PE firms bought insurers and redirected their portfolios into
   private credit that the PE firm itself originates, manages, values and charges
   fees on. The named example: **Global Atlantic, Des Moines, acquired by KKR in
   2021.** *"All backed by retirement savings of ordinary policyholders."*
2. **`SRC-P-003`** — thirty Iowa IPOs, heavily concentrated in **financial
   services and insurance**: Principal, Transamerica/Aegon, Heartland, Norwest,
   American Republic, West Banco.
3. **Seth works at Retirement Income Strategies, in Iowa**, client-facing, in
   exactly the product category those portfolios back.

**The thesis:** *Iowa is a retirement-and-insurance capital centre, and private
credit is quietly reshaping what sits behind those products. I work on the
client side of that and studied the investor side of it.*

That is a genuine, defensible specialization — current, local, and personally
grounded. It is also a **legitimate conversation starter with CVC and private
capital teams**, and directly relevant to insurance-linked investors.
