### Slide 50
VALUATIONS IN PRIVATE MARKETS (VC AND PE)-
VALUATIONS ARE NOT SET BY A MARKET PRICE PER SE
• Every round or deal of a company is done at a different, re-
negotiated price
• Price per share is set and negotiated in Term Sheets.
• It is based on….lots of things…factual and not….
• the dynamics of the company
• the market conditions
• the timeline of the investment funds
• Other terms like Liquidation Preferences

### Slide 51
VENTURE CAPITALIST INVEST VIA A “TERM SHEET”
• We are going to learn all aspects of a real TERM SHEET.
• This is where the price and terms for PREFERRED STOCK are
negotiated
• Investors buy Preferred Stock that has rights above the Common
Stock that set up the company (founders stock)
• My observation: Not understanding the Term Sheet is the NUMBER
ONE source of later issues with founders and investors being
misaligned, especially on EXITS
