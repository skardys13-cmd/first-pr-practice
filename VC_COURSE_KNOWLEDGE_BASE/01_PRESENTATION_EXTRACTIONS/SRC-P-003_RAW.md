VALUATIONS IN PRIVATE MARKETS (VC AND PE)-
VALUATIONS ARE NOT SET BY A MARKET PRICE PER SE
• Every round or deal of a company is done at a different, re-
negotiated price
• Price per share is set and negotiated in Term Sheets.
• It is based on….lots of things…factual and not….
• the dynamics of the company
• the market conditions
• the timeline of the investment funds
• Other terms like Liquidation Preferences

### Slide 33
UN-PRICED ROUNDS
• PRICE CAN BE DEFERRED UNTIL THE NEXT ROUND
• That means it’s a “CONVERTIBLE” Security. There are two types:
• CONVERTIBLE DEBT- which is a loan….
• With typically no payments made
• Dividends accrue…. but are also typically not paid
• SIMPLE AGREEMENT FOR EQUITY- which is a Warrant
• It gives the investor the right to purchase shares of a company's stock at a future date at a
predetermined price

### Slide 34
CONVERTIBLE NOTE (or VENTURE DEBT) option
• The company signs an agreement to “borrow”
money temporarily
• It buys an OPTION to CONVERT to PREFERRED
SHARES and the FUTURE ROUND price
• Until paid or converted, it is a debt, and the is
senior to Preferred Shares, so it is paid back first
• It DOES NOT require a COMPANY VALUATION to
set PRICE…..there is no price yet…..until the next
round

### Slide 35

• It’s a “warrant” (agreed right) to purchase Equity at a
later date

Simple
discounted version)
Agreement
For
• Its term is indefinite.
Equity
• It DOES NOT requires a COMPANY VALUATION
• Very Simple, Very Fast (why it is attractive)
