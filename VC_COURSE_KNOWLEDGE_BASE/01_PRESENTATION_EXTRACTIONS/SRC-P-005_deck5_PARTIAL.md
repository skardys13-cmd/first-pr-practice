UNDERSTANDING VESTING: KEY POINTS  

Employee retention: Vesting periods serve as a powerful tool for companies to retain valuable employees by tying a portion of their compensation to the company's long-term success.

Long-term incentives: The vesting period encourages employees to focus on the company's long-term growth as their equity compensation increases in tandem with the company's value.

Financial planning: Employees need to be aware of their vesting periods to plan for tax implications and other financial considerations related to their equity compensation.

Typical Stock Vesting Schedule for Startup Employees

ACCELERATED VESTING AND CONVERSION

Acceleration: is the clause by which if the liquidity event occurs, the share vesting is accelerated. There are different types of acceleration depending on the % of the vesting to be accelerated (full, partial).

Liquidity event: in ESOP vesting schedules or in [shareholders' agreements](https://www.capboard.io/en/captable/shareholder-agreement-clauses), vesting accelerates or it is converted into shares when a liquidity event occurs. A liquidity event is usually an IPO (going public), an acquisition, or a merger with another company.
