“COMMERCIAL” DUE DILIGENCE- Typical Items  

Market Sizing and Growth Trends: Investigate target segments to confirm actual market size and estimate growth rates. Industry reports and market research offer valuable insight into emerging trends.

Competitive Landscape: Profile major competitors, noting differentiators, potential threats, or saturation points. Independent interviews with customers can highlight the target’s market reputation.

Customer Mix Analysis: Understand revenue concentration, loyalty patterns, and product adoption rates. Heavy reliance on a handful of customers can create vulnerability if a large account leaves.

Pricing Strategies and Value Proposition: Evaluate how pricing aligns with customer value. Spot potential mismatches that hinder expansion or open avenues for premium pricing.

”FINANCIAL” DUE DILIGENCE- FOUR EXAMPLES

Quality of Earnings (QoE): Analyze revenue streams to distinguish recurring revenue from one-time or seasonal sources. Evaluate gross margins, operating expenses, and cost of goods sold to verify the sustainability of profit margins.

Working Capital Requirements: Determine whether the target maintains sufficient liquidity (cash) to support operations. Excessive reliance on short-term debt can escalate financial risk. (EV + WC = TOTAL NEEDED TO BUY AND RUN)
