The thing that actually gets you hired

Portfolio vault

Certifications prove you studied. A portfolio proves you operate. Every item here is a file with a link, a one-line result and a number — tick it when it exists and someone outside your head could open it. Starting this in month one is the whole advantage: you are documenting each process the week you are taught it, which means by month twelve you will own the documentation for a job you were still learning in January.

Compliance guardrail, read once and never forget it: nothing in this portfolio contains real client data. No names, no account numbers, no balances, no statements, no screenshots of Schwab Advisor Center or Redtail with live records. You build every artifact on synthetic data you generate yourself, and you clear anything public with your firm's compliance contact first. A single PII leak ends this plan and your career in the industry — and reviewers are more impressed by a clean synthetic dataset than by a redacted real one.

The destination

      
Chief Operating Officer

      
An RIA COO owns operations, the firm’s economics, its people and its projects. This tab is the map of that seat, what this plan does and does not build toward it, and the three ways people actually get there. Targets are mid-size RIAs at $1–5b, the platform layer, and the acquirers — with pay weighted ahead of title.

    

    
The part no plan fixes: you cannot build your way to this seat. Documentation, reporting and automation compress the first two steps hard — Operations Manager and Director of Operations are evaluated on exactly that work, and arriving with a real record takes years off each. The last step is gated on two things a portfolio cannot supply: having managed people, and having owned a number. Blocks 53 and 54 get you as close to both as is possible from outside the role.

The number is the point

      
Compensation model

      
$80k+ on the move, $100k at year two, $130–150k at years four to six, inside 40–50 hours. Anchored on the step you already have in front of you: Associate Advisor at around $65k, three to six months out, gated on the Series 65. That is a real internal track and it changes the arithmetic — you are not applying into your next band from outside, you are being promoted into it and then moving with the title already on the resume.
