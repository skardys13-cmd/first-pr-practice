Operations capacity & economics

A model of how much operations work this firm carries, who carries it, what it costs, and when it runs out of room. Every number carries a tag saying where it came from. Most of them are still guesses, and the model says so on its face.

Rules this model runs under

No client data of any kind is in this file. The model works on counts, tiers, ranges and averages; the smallest unit anywhere in it is a tier of at least eighteen households. There are no names, no account numbers, no balances, no statements.

Nothing here is a compliance opinion. Compliance ownership stays with the firm's designated person. Where the model touches a compliance calendar item it is counting minutes, not judging adequacy.

No credential, password or vendor login appears in this model or in the workbook. The published version names no person, no firm, no custodian and no vendor — the labels table is anonymous by default and the toggle above is how it stays that way.
